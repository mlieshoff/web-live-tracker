    Capture guide

    1. Enter a research ID and choose Start capture. Use the inspected tab to generate the requests you want to save.
    2. Choose Stop capture and wait for the files and report to finish saving.
    3. Find your files in your browser’s Downloads folder under web-live-tracker, then your research ID. Capture files are grouped by date.
    4. Choose Open capture report. If prompted, select your research folder to include earlier captures.
